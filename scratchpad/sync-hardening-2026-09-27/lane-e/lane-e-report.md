# Lane E — SFU options for Pakistan and the Middle East

**Decision date:** 27 September 2026. **Scope:** desk research and read-only Chalk code inspection; no service signup, purchase, deployment, or live probe. Provider prices are public list prices in USD, not quotes. Space/Episode/Participant are Chalk concepts; vendor “room,” “session,” and “track” terms below are confined to adapter descriptions.

## Decision in one page

**Trial only two alternatives:** (1) **LiveKit Cloud**, using its India and Middle East region groups and default multi-home mesh; (2) **LiveKit OSS, single-home in UAE**, with Mumbai and Singapore as measured counterfactuals, not a three-region rollout. Both share a substantially similar SDK/API, so the second trial measures the value and burden of self-hosting rather than requiring a second Chalk integration. LiveKit Cloud is the stronger likely production answer for mixed Karachi/Gulf Spaces: its published realtime locations include **Mumbai, South India, UAE and Saudi Arabia**, and its Cloud-only architecture connects each Participant to a near edge and meshes SFUs. LiveKit OSS is a credible cost/control option but **a Space stays on one media node** even in its distributed multi-region mode. [LiveKit 2026 region list](https://docs.livekit.io/deploy/admin/regions/endpoints/), [Cloud versus OSS architecture](https://docs.livekit.io/intro/cloud/), [OSS distributed mode](https://docs.livekit.io/transport/self-hosting/distributed/).

**Do not treat Cloudflare RealtimeKit/Dyte as an independent escape route.** RealtimeKit uses Cloudflare's realtime infrastructure, and Dyte SDKs are maintenance-mode predecessors. The existing Cloudflare Realtime SFU remains the price benchmark, but the Karachi control-path observation (p50 1.7 s, p95 9.5 s, 503s and 10 s timeouts) is **user-supplied measurement, not reproduced in this lane**. Also, the camera-off/on remote-video failure must be fixed or isolated before attributing all missing video to a provider. [RealtimeKit overview](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/realtimekit/), [Dyte migration notice](https://docs.dyte.io/guides/realtimekit-migration/sdk-guide).

**Important evidence limit:** I could not substantiate a four-origin, cloud-endpoint RTT matrix from a current measured source. WonderNetwork lists Karachi, Dubai, Riyadh, Muscat, Mumbai, Singapore, Doha and Manama and explains its 30-ping averages, but direct city-pair fetches returned `Failed to fetch https://wondernetwork.com/pings/Karachi/Dubai: (502) Bad Gateway`; a direct `curl` returned `curl: (28) Resolving timed out after 12005 milliseconds`. Consequently the RTT ranges below are **planning hypotheses**, not measurements or procurement evidence. This is exactly what the trial must resolve. [WonderNetwork methodology](https://wondernetwork.com/pings/), [locations](https://wondernetwork.com/locations).

## Comparable workload and arithmetic

One Participant-hour means one Participant connected for one hour: 1,000/10,000 Participant-hours = 60,000/600,000 Participant-minutes = 250/2,500 four-Participant Space-hours. **Model assumption:** each Participant publishes 1280×720 video at **1.5 Mb/s** and 64 kb/s audio and receives all other three at those rates, continuously. Thus downstream per Participant is `3 × (1.5 + .064) Mb/s = 4.692 Mb/s = 2.1114 decimal GB/hour`; totals are **2,111/21,114 GB/month**. This is deliberately a high 720p all-visible case, not a measured Chalk bitrate; simulcast selection, mute, screenshare, retransmissions, TURN paths, recording, storage, taxes, minimums, and peak-capacity headroom can change bills. Per-minute products ignore that bandwidth assumption unless they tier by resolution. Cross-region cascading can add backbone transfer, not included.

### Self-hosted SFU software

All self-host estimates share the **same ~2.1/21.1 TB public downstream demand**; software licences themselves are $0, but server, public egress, TURN relay, recording compute/storage, monitoring, failover, and engineering are extra. For an illustrative **DigitalOcean Bangalore or Singapore** deployment only: one $48/month 4-vCPU/8-GiB VM with 5,000 GiB included transfer covers the 1k-hour arithmetic; two such VMs contribute 10,000 GiB at 10k hours and leave about 9,666 GiB × $0.01 ≈ $97 overage, or **~$193/month compute + public egress**. This is **not a capacity or HA quote**: 4 vCPUs are shared, peaks and TURN can require more nodes, and the 10k-hour average is only ~14 concurrent Participants. [Droplet price/allowance](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets), [bandwidth pooling](https://docs.digitalocean.com/platform/billing/bandwidth/).

## Chalk integration impact (read from current tree)

The intended neutral seams exist, but **the current signaling contract is not provider-neutral enough for a one-file swap**:

1. **Domain and server port.** [theory.md](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/theory.md:14) says MediaPlane owns provider details. [mediaplane/service.go](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/apps/api/internal/mediaplane/service.go:25) exposes Episode/Join/Usage and opaque client payload, which is reusable. But [mediaplane/signaling.go](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/apps/api/internal/mediaplane/signaling.go:11) exposes Cloudflare-style `location`, `mid`, `trackName`, `sessionId`, and SDP add/close/renegotiate; it should become a provider-selected signaling port or be replaced for LiveKit WebSocket token/SDK signaling. Current Cloudflare `EpisodeUsage` returns metadata, **not** real Participant-minutes/egress; cost observability must be implemented in a new adapter, not assumed. [Cloudflare adapter](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/apps/api/internal/adapters/cloudflare/sfu/adapter.go:821).
2. **Join/bootstrap and TURN.** Cloudflare adapter creates a provider session by HTTP `POST /sessions/new`, returns its ID plus `stun:stun.cloudflare.com:3478` in the client payload, and calls `/sessions/{id}/tracks/new`, `/tracks/close`, `/renegotiate` with backend-held App Secret. [adapter.go](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/apps/api/internal/adapters/cloudflare/sfu/adapter.go:231). LiveKit needs Episode→vendor Room mapping, short-lived Participant token with grants, a WSS endpoint, regional routing/pinning choice, ICE/TURN configuration via its SDK/service, and disconnect/cleanup and usage semantics. No Cloudflare App Secret should reach the browser.
3. **Browser SDK.** [media/client.ts](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/sdks/typescript/client/src/media/client.ts:1) is Cloudflare-specific behind a small [ClientMediaPlane interface](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/sdks/typescript/client/src/media/plane.ts:1). It currently sends explicit local/remote track requests, parses `chalk_pub_v1` provider reference into Cloudflare connection ID + track name, polls a versioned publication snapshot, maps responses to transceiver MIDs, and handles SDP/renegotiation. [tracks.ts](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/sdks/typescript/client/src/media/tracks.ts:9), [transport.ts](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/sdks/typescript/client/src/media/transport.ts:4), [types.ts](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/sdks/typescript/client/src/media/types.ts:6). A LiveKit client adapter should use its event-driven publication/subscription model and stable Chalk `(Participant, source, generation)` identity, while preserving `setLocalPublicationTarget`, observer callbacks, idempotence/recovery and telemetry. Avoid translating LiveKit events through fake Cloudflare `tracks/new` calls.
4. **Capture and recording.** The generic [CapturePlane](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/apps/api/internal/captureplane/types.go:1) has opaque provider references, revision/epoch/idempotency fencing and SDP negotiation. Its Cloudflare implementation translates owner reference→publisher session ID, track reference→track name, selected simulcast layer→`h/m/l` RID, validates returned MIDs, and pulls tracks into a separate recorder connection. [capture_plane.go](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/apps/api/internal/adapters/cloudflare/sfu/capture_plane.go:386). The [recorder coordinator](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/apps/api/internal/recordercapture/coordinator.go:654) reconciles desired tracks; the [Pion worker](/Users/macmini/code/chalk/apps/api/internal/recorderworker/capture_attempt.go:206) waits for MIDs/first RTP, writes packet bundles and renders/transcribes later. An alternative must either (a) implement CapturePlane with a compatible Pion subscriber and track/codec/MID mapping, **or** (b) ingest provider egress into Chalk's bundle/artifact lifecycle. “Provider offers recording” is not enough to preserve Chalk Recording behavior. Native LiveKit Egress is promising, but a provider recording plus webhooks is a **separate qualification** for epoch, plan revision, loss/retry, artifact and Transcript parity.
5. **Existing defect.** The user-supplied camera-off/on failure—remote `POST .../media/sfu/tracks` success/retry without a following renegotiate—crosses the Cloudflare browser/server signaling seam. Reproducing it on LiveKit would test a different event-driven model; it does **not** diagnose or fix the current defect. Keep its regression case provider-independent at the publication/remote-first-frame level.

**Sizing inference:** a _trial-quality_ LiveKit Cloud/OSS path is roughly **2–3 engineering weeks**: Go join/token/config/selection + browser ClientMediaPlane adapter + four-Participant video/recovery harness. A _production-qualification_ path with CapturePlane/Recording parity, usage accounting, health/observability, hardening and migration is more like **6–10 engineering weeks** for one experienced engineer (order-of-magnitude, not a quote). The reusable LiveKit Cloud→OSS client and token protocol reduces the second deployment's code, not its networking/operational qualification. mediasoup/Janus/Jitsi/Pion likely exceed this because Chalk must build more signaling or orchestration.

## Trial design and gates

**Sequence:** (A) first reproduce the current baseline and camera-off/on regression on the local development stack, with identical browser/camera/test media; (B) implement the one LiveKit client+server adapter behind configuration, with Cloud and OSS endpoints; (C) test Cloud in default mesh and India/ME pinned modes, then OSS single-home UAE versus Mumbai/Singapore. No signup or spending occurred in this research lane; this is a proposed future trial.

Run 4-person Spaces in Karachi-only, Gulf-only, and 2 Karachi + 2 Gulf mixes.
