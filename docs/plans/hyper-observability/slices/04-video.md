# Slice 04: Video

This slice proves that a Participant's camera reaches every viewer as a moving, sharp picture, and names the first hop where it stopped when it doesn't. SWE-2 builds it. It owns the `video.` prefix and covers camera video only, from capture to the rendered tile. It depends on C1 (registry, envelope, `transition`/`op`/`fact`/`surface`/`decision`), C2 (`ProofBundle`, three-state values, the three verdict fields), C3 (`evidence_gap`, source watermarks) and C4 (compact lines, `--json`). It reads, and never redefines, slice 1's admission record, slice 2's media connection state, slice 7's reconnect and background transitions, slice 8's end record and slice 9's crash records. Code facts are at b3dc1d3afa.

Journeys, on web and mobile: an admitted Participant turns the camera on and it starts; the camera keeps running until they turn it off; every viewer whose tile is on screen sees that camera moving; a viewer with a large tile on a clean link gets the high layer.

Boundaries. A camera failure in the prejoin preview is slice 1. A capture failure inside the join step (`acquireInitialMedia` at `sdks/typescript/client/src/connection/lifecycle.ts:569`) is slice 2's, because it runs before the media connection is connected. Media connection setup, ICE and listen-only are slice 2: no `video.` rule fires while either side's media connection is not connected. Audio is slice 3. Screen share video is slice 5: every rule keeps only `source=camera`. A source with no live grant (`source_unauthorized`) and reconnect after network loss are slice 7. A crashed tab is slice 9. Recorder frame presence is slice 10, read as an optional third witness and never required.

## 2. Symptom catalogue

| Code | Meaning | Who is affected | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| `video.camera_wont_start` | The admitted Participant turned the camera on and capture failed or never went live. | the publisher | medium |
| `video.camera_stopped` | A live camera ended or went muted at the source without the Participant turning it off. | the publisher and all their viewers | medium |
| `video.not_published` | The camera is live and bound to a connected media connection, but no camera packets reach the SFU. | all viewers of the publisher | medium |
| `video.not_shown` | A viewer's on-screen tile shows no picture of a camera whose packets reach the SFU. | one viewer | medium |
| `video.frozen` | A viewer's picture stops moving for seconds at a time. | one viewer, or all viewers when the publisher side caused it | medium |
| `video.low_quality` | A viewer gets the camera starved (under 100 kbps) or on the low layer on a large tile. | one viewer, or all viewers when the publisher side caused it | low |

Hops, in chain order: `client_capture`, `client_encode`, `publisher_uplink`, `viewer_selection`, `sfu_forward` (`cf_forward` on Cloudflare), `subscriber_downlink`, `client_decode`, `client_render`, and `unknown`. A finding on `client_capture`, `client_encode` or `publisher_uplink` names the publisher and appends `viewers=<n>`; any other hop names the viewer and appends `from=<publisher handle>`. Module per hop, unless a rule below says otherwise:

| Hop | client_capture | client_encode | publisher_uplink | viewer_selection | sfu_forward / cf_forward | subscriber_downlink | client_decode | client_render | unknown |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Module | `sdk.media` (`sdk.camera_effects` when a camera-effect failure is recorded) | `sdk.media` | `network` | `sdk.media` | `sfu.media` / `cf.sfu` | `network` | `sdk.media` | `react.tile` (web), `rn.tile` (native) | `unknown` |

## 4. Rule conditions

Missing data never yields healthy. A rule returns `broken`, `degraded`, `expected` or a gap; healthy comes only from the sealed `ProofBundle`. Shared terms:

- *Admitted*: slice 1's admission record for the Participant. *Connected*: slice 2's media connection state is connected for that Participant throughout the stretch.
- *Bound*: the `camera` transition's latest state is `live` with `bound=true`.
- *Flowing*: an SFU ingress or subscription row whose packet count grew by >= 50 since the previous row of the same track, with the rows <= 35 s apart (`scripts/diagnose-call/quality.mjs:7`). *Not flowing*: a row with growth < 50, or `packets=0` on the first row, followed by no flowing row for 20 s. No row after it counts as `observed(0)` only up to the `sfu` source watermark; past the watermark it is a gap.
- *Window*: one 10 s `call_stats` sample (`sdks/typescript/client/src/media/call-stats.ts:9`). A window carrying `gap` (`docs/observability.md:81`) measured nothing.
- *On screen*: the probe reports `on_screen=true`, `page_visible=true`, `covered=false` and `rect_h >= 36`.
- *Older SDK*: a Participant who sends `call_stats` but no `camera` transition or `video.render_probe` rows. Rules needing those rows return `partial(client)` or `partial(client.render)`; no transition never means no failure.
- Exclusions: no rule fires while either side is not connected, during a slice 9 crash record for the affected tab, while the publisher's camera subscription reads `pause_reason=source_unauthorized` or has `sfu.source.lease_lost source=camera` (`features/media/sfu/internal/media/authority.go:98`, `:235`) with no later `sfu.source.lease_opened` (slice 7), or for `source=screen`.
- One code per publisher, viewer and stretch, the first in this order: `camera_wont_start`, `camera_stopped`, `not_published`, `not_shown`, `frozen`, `low_quality`.
- Gap coverage strings: `partial(sfu)` (SFU rows absent on a Chalk SFU Episode or past its watermark), `partial(cf)` (Cloudflare, `scripts/diagnose-call/gaps.mjs:27`), `partial(client)` (the named Participant's client rows absent, windows carrying `gap`, or an older SDK), `partial(client.render)` (probe absent, or `unavailable(native_render)`).

## 7. Out of scope and traps

Don't touch: other prefixes and their files (`join.`, `media.`, `audio.`, `share.`, `access.`, `reconnect.`, `background.`, `end.`, `page.`, `app.`, `perf.`, `recording.`); `apps/api/internal/adapters/cloudflare/sfu/*` (read only; the in-flight branch `origin/obs/cf-per-track-error-code` changes `apps/api/internal/adapters/cloudflare/sfu/failure_observability.go:43`, and this slice must not depend on it); `scripts/diagnose-call/verdict.mjs`; the foundation contracts; production config, alert thresholds and `infrastructure/observability/production-alerts/call-verdict.json` values.

Migration. `starved_viewer` and `congested_viewer` (both from `lowBitrate`, `scripts/diagnose-call/rules.mjs:177`) become `video.low_quality`. `viewer_hid_video` (`hiddenVideo`, `scripts/diagnose-call/rules.mjs:213`) becomes the `expected(viewer_hidden)` branch of `video.not_shown`. Remove the three from `KINDS` (`scripts/diagnose-call/rules.mjs:253`) and call `scripts/diagnose-call/video.mjs (new)` from `findingsOf` (`scripts/diagnose-call/rules.mjs:282`). Move `starvedViewerCall`, `starvedWithoutNodeCall`, `hiddenByViewerCall` and `congestedViewerCall` (`scripts/diagnose-call/scenarios.mjs:112`, `:125`, `:181`, `:202`) into `scripts/diagnose-call/scenarios-video.mjs (new)` and update their imports. `uplink_degraded`, `node_dropping` and `downlink_degraded` stay where they are, as hop evidence.

Traps a literal builder will hit:
- `useVideoTrack` status `playing`, `loadeddata` or a resolved `video.play()` (`sdks/typescript/react/src/components/participant-tile/useVideoTrack.ts:51`) is not a picture. Only the probe's presented frames, geometry, `covered` and luma prove one.
- `sfu.subscription.paused reason=viewer`, `recv_hidden_videos` and the IntersectionObserver report are one decision. Never count one as corroborating another.
- A 200 from `cloudflare_sfu.add_tracks`, a `subscribe` `media_result succeeded` (`sdks/typescript/client/src/media/client.ts:1883`) or `sfu.publication.started` means a request or negotiation succeeded, not that packets flowed.
- No ingress row after a no-growth row is `observed(0)` only up to the `sfu` watermark. Transport rows don't extend it. Past it is `evidence_gap`, never healthy.
- A `call_stats` window with `gap` is not zero packets. A late or hidden probe tick is not a black tile. A Participant with no camera transitions is an older SDK, not a camera that never failed.
- A reconnect that starts after the picture stopped, or with cause `receive_stalled`, is the stall's consequence and never makes a finding EXPECTED.
- `rect_h` is in device pixels, like the viewer layer policy; comparing CSS pixels to 480 misreads every high-DPI tile.
- Never print healthy because no rule fired; `scripts/diagnose-call/verdict.mjs:19` does that today. Video results go through the `ProofBundle` only.
- Don't wrap every media function in `op`, proxy `RTCPeerConnection` globally in the SDK, or add timers beyond the probe. Use only the boundaries in section 5.
