# Slice 05: Screen share

This slice proves four things about a screen share: it goes live when a Participant picks a screen, every other Participant sees a moving picture of it, it stops when the sharer stops it, and every refusal had a real cause. SWE-2 builds it, and it owns the `share.` prefix. It depends on C1 (registry, kernel calls, handles), C2 (ProofBundle, three-state values, verdict fields), C3 (`evidence_gap`, source watermarks) and C4 (compact grammar). It reuses slice 4's render measurement (`video.render_probe`, 04-video.md section 5) on the share element and adds no render fields of its own, and it relies on slice 2 for the media connection. Code facts are at b3dc1d3afa. An Episode has one share at a time: Sync's lease refuses a second sharer with `screen_share_in_use` (`apps/sync/lib/chalk_sync/stateholder/postgres/screen_share_lease.ex:53`), and the SFU separately refuses a second screen track with `screen_share_active` (`features/media/sfu/internal/media/node_create.go:165-166`).

## 2. Symptom catalogue

| Code | Meaning | Who is affected | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| `share.start_failed` | The sharer picked a screen, but the share never went live for the Episode. | sharer, all viewers | medium |
| `share.capture_failed` | The screen picker failed for a reason other than the Participant's own cancel or an OS refusal. | sharer | medium |
| `share.blocked` | A Participant was refused with `screen_share_in_use` while nobody was really sharing. | the refused Participant | medium |
| `share.not_seen` | The share is live at the SFU, but a present viewer has no visible picture of it. | one viewer | medium |
| `share.frozen` | The viewer's share is on screen and packets arrive, but no new frame is presented. | one viewer | medium |
| `share.wont_stop` | The sharer asked to stop, and 5 s later Sync hasn't stopped it, the SFU still receives it, or a viewer still shows it. | sharer, all viewers | high |

`share.wont_stop` is high by decision: the Participant can't stop others seeing their screen, which is a privacy exposure. A share that ends without the sharer asking gets no code: every browser end arrives as `ended`, and the SDK treats it as the Participant's own stop (`sdks/typescript/client/src/space-client/media-controller.ts:1618`), so the two can't be told apart. A problem report about it becomes `BLIND_SPOT`.

## 4. Rule conditions

Missing data never yields healthy. A rule returns `broken`, `degraded`, `expected` or a gap; only the sealed ProofBundle yields `healthy`. Thresholds live in `infrastructure/observability/production-alerts/call-verdict.json`, and every key is read by `scripts/diagnose-call/share.mjs (new)`: `share_ingress_ms=25000`, `share_view_ms=10000`, `share_stop_ms=5000`, `share_lease_stale_ms=35000` (20 s renewal, `apps/sync/lib/chalk_sync/stateholder/postgres/screen_share_lease.ex:7`, plus the 15 s look, `apps/sync/lib/chalk_sync/episodes/coordinator/server.ex:17`), `share_owner_live_ms=20000`, `share_picker_min_ms=300`, `share_role_race_ms=10000`, `share_frozen_ms=5000`, `share_frozen_packets=30`.

Shared terms. **grew(O, [a, b])**: an ingress line for O with `source=screen` timed in [a, b] has `packets` above O's previous line for that `mid`. **flat(O, [a, b])**: no such line and the sfu watermark is complete to b or later; without the watermark it is a gap `partial(sfu)`. **Present viewer V**: W12 has V joined before a and not left before b, V is not O, and V's client watermark is complete to b; a present V with no client rows is a gap `partial(client)`. **O asked to stop at t**: a W15 `share` transition `on→stopping` for O with trigger `user_control` or `platform_ended` at t. A W2 `share_stop` alone is not asking, because a long drop also sends it (`sdks/typescript/client/src/space-client/media-controller.ts:1522`, `:1535-1538`). **visible(V)**: a W11 probe for O's screen publication with `on_screen=true`, `page_visible=true`, `covered=false`, `video_width>0` and `presented_frames` grown since V's previous probe.

**`share.start_failed`.** t is W1 `ok` for O; the window is [t, t+`share_ingress_ms`].
- Not applicable when, inside the window, O asked to stop, O left (W12), a newer `share.capture` started, or O's `sfu.peer.state` (`features/media/sfu/internal/media/events.go:30`) was not connected at any point (slice 2's symptom).
- BROKEN, first match sets hop and module: (1) W3 `share.start` is not ok with a reason other than `screen_share_in_use`: `hop=sync_publication module=sync.publication`; (2) W3 ok and W7 refused O: `hop=sfu_admission module=sfu.screen_claim`, plus the disagreement `sync.share_start=ok` vs `sfu.share_publish=<code>`; (3) W3 ok and no `sfu.publication.started source=screen` for O in the window, with the sfu watermark complete to its end: `hop=client_publish module=sdk.media`; (4) publication started and flat(O, window): `hop=client_capture`, `module=ios.broadcast_extension` when O's client platform is iOS, else `module=sdk.media`.
- EXPECTED only when W3's reason is `capability_denied` and W14 has `role_assigned` for O in [t-`share_role_race_ms`, t]. Without that role change, `capability_denied` is leg 1.
- Gaps: no W3 but W2 carries the passed-through code: run leg 1 on W2's code, coverage `partial(sync)`. Neither: `partial(sync)`. Legs 2 to 4 without sfu evidence: `partial(sfu)`.

**`share.capture_failed`.** Every W1 error maps to one cause. BROKEN causes: `policy_blocked` (`display_capture_allowed=observed(false)`; `hop=host_permissions_policy`, `module=tenant.embed_policy` when `framed=observed(true)`, `module=web.headers` when `framed=observed(false)`); `refused_without_picker` (`NotAllowedError`, `duration_ms` below `share_picker_min_ms`, no `permission_block=system`, `display_capture_allowed` is `observed(true)` or `not_applicable`); `no_user_activation` (`InvalidStateError`); `unsupported` (`NotSupportedError`, `SecurityError`); `bad_constraints` (`OverconstrainedError`, `TypeError`); `capture_device` (`NotReadableError`, `NotFoundError`, `AbortError`, `OperationError`, `unknown`). All but `policy_blocked` are `hop=client_capture module=sdk.media`.
- EXPECTED: `os_permission` when `permission_block=system` (Chrome's "by system" refusal, `sdks/typescript/client/src/media/capture-error.ts:26-29`); `picker_cancelled` when `NotAllowedError` arrives after `share_picker_min_ms` or more with no `permission_block=system`, because a policy block rejects before any picker.
- Gap: `NotAllowedError` faster than `share_picker_min_ms` with `display_capture_allowed=unavailable(no_policy_api)` (no `document.featurePolicy`, as in Safari and Firefox) yields `partial(client.policy)`, never a cancel.

**`share.blocked`.** t is a W3 `share.start` refusal of X with `screen_share_in_use`. The owner O is the Participant whose last W4 transition before t left the lease `held` (a `held→released` whose `reason` isn't `ok` leaves it held); when W4 is missing, O is W5's `owner_participant_id`; with neither, the gap is `partial(sync)`.
- BROKEN, `hop=sync_lease module=sync.screen_lease`, when (a) O asked to stop before t and W3 `share.stop` for that stop was ok, or W4 shows O's release with a reason other than `ok`; or (b) flat(O, [t-`share_lease_stale_ms`, t]).
- EXPECTED `owner_live` when grew(O, [t-`share_owner_live_ms`, t]) and O did not ask to stop between O's lease acquire and t. EXPECTED `lease_draining` when O's last growth falls in [t-`share_lease_stale_ms`, t-`share_owner_live_ms`) and O never asked to stop.
- Cloudflare: (a) still applies, coverage `partial(sfu)`. (b) is replaced by "no present viewer has a W10 `share.view` for O in any state but `ended` in [t-`share_lease_stale_ms`, t]"; a present viewer with no client rows makes it `partial(client)` with no outcome. EXPECTED is never reached there: `partial(sfu)`.

**`share.not_seen`.** The window W is [a, a+`share_view_ms`], with a at least `share_view_ms` after O's `sfu.publication.started source=screen`, and grew(O) in every ingress interval of W. BROKEN when a present viewer V has no visible(V) probe in W and no EXPECTED case holds. First match sets hop and module: (1) V's `share.view` is mounted and its probes show `rect_w=0`, `rect_h=0`, `on_screen=false` or `covered=true` with `page_visible=true`: `hop=client_render module=react.share_view`; the SDK then stops pulling a tile it thinks is off screen (`sdks/typescript/client/src/media/video-tile-visibility.ts:8`), so a W8 `paused reason=viewer` after that is a consequence, not the hop; (2) no W8 subscription for V on O's screen, or W8 paused or stopped with a reason other than `viewer`: `hop=sfu_forward module=sfu.forward`; (3) W9 `packets` on that `mid` is 0 in W: `hop=client_receive module=sdk.media`; (4) otherwise: `hop=client_render module=react.share_view`.
- EXPECTED when: W13 shows V `hidden` or `background` from before a to after W and every probe in W says `page_visible=false`; W10 `pip=true` (the share video is the picture-in-picture element); W10 `hidden=true` with `pinned_other=true` set by V's own pin (`sdks/typescript/react/src/components/stage/Stage.tsx:295`) before a; or W8 `paused reason=congestion` for V on O's screen while V's `recv_bitrate_kbps` in the same W9 window is below `starved_floor_kbps`. A `hidden=true` with no pin by V is leg 1.
- Gaps: a native V has `presented_frames=unavailable(native_render)`, giving `partial(client.render)`; Cloudflare gives `partial(sfu)`.

**`share.frozen`.** BROKEN, `hop=client_render module=react.share_view`, when V's W10 `share.view` for O is `live` or `low-quality` with `pip=false`; in one W9 window with `window_ms` of 8000 or more and no `gap`, `packets` on O's screen `mid` grew by `share_frozen_packets` or more; and two W11 probes in that window, `share_frozen_ms` or more apart, both with `on_screen=true` and `page_visible=true`, show no `presented_frames` growth between them. There is no EXPECTED case, because those preconditions already exclude a hidden page, picture-in-picture and a paused share. With growth below `share_frozen_packets` the rule makes no claim: a still screen sends almost nothing, which is a stated blind spot. Decided: the rule never reads `freeze_ms` for a share, because screen content's uneven frame rate makes it fire on a still screen. Gaps: a native V or no probe for an on-screen share gives `partial(client.render)`; Cloudflare has no `mid` join, giving `partial(sfu)`.

**`share.wont_stop`.** t is O asking to stop. Not applicable when the Episode ended or O left in [t, t+`share_stop_ms`], or when the transition's trigger is `moderation_pause` and W14 has `participant_screen_share_stopped` for O.
- BROKEN, first match sets hop and module: (1) W3 `share.stop` for this stop is not ok, or absent by t+`share_stop_ms` with the sync watermark complete: `hop=sync_publication module=sync.publication`; (2) grew(O) in an ingress interval whose previous line is at or after t+`share_stop_ms`: `hop=client_publish module=sdk.media`; (3) a present viewer V's last `share.view` state for O before t+`share_stop_ms` is anything but `ended`: `hop=client_render module=react.share_view`.
- EXPECTED for leg 3 only: V's W13 `sync_socket_close` is before t and its next `sync_socket_open` is after t+`share_stop_ms`. That stale tile is slice 7's.
- Gaps: leg 1 without sync rows is `partial(sync)`; leg 2 without sfu is `partial(sfu)`; a present V with no client rows is `partial(client)`.

## 6. Fault scenarios

## 7. Out of scope and traps

**Don't touch.** Other slices' prefixes, `sdks/typescript/react/src/components/participant-tile/useVideoTrack.ts`, `sdks/typescript/client/src/media/call-stats.ts`, `sdks/typescript/client/src/media/client.ts`, slice 4's probe files, the camera and microphone paths in `sdks/typescript/client/src/space-client/media-controller.ts`, `ScreenSharePresentation`, `StageContentTileSurface`, `sdks/typescript/react/src/components/space-view/RecordingSpaceView.tsx`, the C1 to C4 contracts and production config. Leave every line named in `tools/formal/screen-share-lease/anchors` byte-identical, including each `_ = release_screen_lease(state, lease)`; that is why the release result is reported inside `release_screen_lease/2`. Run `tools/formal/screen-share-lease/run.sh` after the Sync change.

**Traps.**
- `media_result share_start outcome=succeeded` means only that the Sync command's Promise resolved (`sdks/typescript/client/src/sync/v1-live-target-telemetry.ts:21-23`). Sync's `live_target.stage` timing always says `outcome: :success` (`apps/sync/lib/chalk_sync/telemetry.ex:27`). Only W6 ingress and the viewers' W11 prove a share is live.
- W2 with the passed-through `reason_code` is a copy of W3, W5 is a copy of W4, Sync's projection is a copy of W6, and `sfu.admission.refused screen_ceiling` is the same decision as `share.publish`. None is a second witness.
- `screen_share_in_use` becomes `command_rejected` on the client (`sdks/typescript/client/src/sync/v1-live-target-telemetry.ts:37-38`). Never build a rule on `command_rejected`.
- `useVideoTrack` reports `playing` after one `loadeddata` (`sdks/typescript/react/src/components/participant-tile/useVideoTrack.ts:31-38`): one decoded frame, not visibility. `framesDecoded` keeps rising behind a paused or hidden element. Visibility comes only from W11.
- `device_failed` records every `NotAllowedError`, a cancel included, as `permission_state=denied` (`sdks/typescript/client/src/media/capture-error.ts:40-43`). Use W1's duration and policy fields.
- An empty SFU query on Cloudflare, no lease row, no viewer rows, or an ingress silence without a complete watermark are gaps. None means "nobody shared" or "everyone saw it".
- Never log `MediaStreamTrack.label`, capture labels or window titles; only the `displaySurface` enum. No SDP, no frame content, no pixels.
- Adopt `op` only at `sdks/typescript/client/src/space-client/media-controller.ts:708`. Don't wrap the Effect pipelines or timers, and don't add an op in the native adapter. A rule never emits backstops and never returns `healthy`.
