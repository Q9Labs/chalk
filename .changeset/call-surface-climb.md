Breaking: `CallReady` and `CallReadyProps` are no longer exported from `@q9labsai/chalk-react`, `CommandErrorAlert` is no longer exported from its `test-support` entry, and `ControlBar` no longer takes `shareUrl`, `callLinkOpen`, `onCallLinkToggle` or `onCopyError`, so a standalone `ControlBar` has no Copy call link button. Pass `callLinkOpen` and `onCallLinkToggle` to `SpaceHeader` or `SpaceView` instead.

The call invite now lives in the call menu. A host who is alone at call start sees the menu open once the call is live, titled "Your call is ready", with the QR code expanded, and it closes when someone joins; a link too long for a QR code says so there. The menu gains "Who can join" and Mute everyone rows for hosts, fed by the new optional `hostControls` prop of `SpaceHeader` (`SpaceView` fills it); `ParticipantsPanel` no longer shows them. The rest of the menu is unchanged. The dock has no link button, and the phone call menu has no Show call link row.

Record sits with the dock's centre controls, keeps its neutral fill, and only its glyph turns red while recording. The header keeps its Rec pill while recording, on a phone as on desktop. A standalone `ControlBar` shows its media notice or command failure as a dismissible error toast. Each half of a split dock button shows its own focus ring, and the phone dock fits at 200% zoom.

The dock and header stay visible while the pointer rests on them, and any click on the stage wakes them. The camera menu and the phone device sheets load on intent, so they open at full height. The call menu waits behind the joining card instead of opening over it.

While the link is lost, the header and the stage keep their last picture under one blur and veil, the reconnecting card sits at the centre of the call, and the dock stays above the veil. The microphone works while reconnecting or offline, and once the connection is lost only Leave stays active while the other dock buttons dim. The "Back in the call" confirmation joins the bottom notices. Connection lost is the same veil and card changing in place into a way back, with Rejoin, Leave call and, when a report can be sent, Report a problem, and no error code; focus returns to the dock when it closes. The error code and its connection detail go into every problem report instead, and the end screen no longer shows the raw reference code either. `reportDiagnostics` (from `@q9labsai/chalk-react`) and `formatFailureReference` (from `@q9labsai/chalk-client`) build those report lines. `CallLimbo` takes a `secondaryAction` for a quieter second way forward beside the main one. The limbo card shows a blurred stand-in call behind it, and its "This call is recorded" line opens its row instead of popping in.

The people panel uses plain mic and camera icons and a host crown, and lists only people. Hosts get "Who can join" and Mute everyone as rows in the call menu instead, and Mute everyone is hidden when you're alone. Someone who is reconnecting never leads the stage unless pinned or alone. A side column of shared content keeps people in view instead of folding them into +N, and on a phone the second face floats at the top right of the big tile, clear of the notices at the bottom. A cancelled screen-share picker no longer adds a share tile. Your own big share drops its repeated tag, and on narrow windows its bar moves to the bottom edge. A long tile name ends in an ellipsis, and the tile signal chip uses the name tag's on-media colours. On a phone or touch screen, a tile's More menu opens as a sheet instead of a hover button, and tapping a tile no longer throws a hooks-order error.

The reactions tray closes on a click anywhere else. A chat message's reaction bar opens at the far edge, away from the sender name and time, a failed attachment says "Try again" instead of "Retry", and the hidden attachment input is out of the tab order. The same board message again replaces its notice instead of stacking a copy.

The call theme uses the shared danger reds, so white text on Leave, Remove and Clear board holds 4.5:1 in both themes.

Some call messages read differently. A host who refuses a knock now shows "The host didn’t let you in", with Ask again and Go to Chalk. A call that can't be entered says "You’re offline. Reconnect, then try again.", "Chalk is having trouble right now. Try again in a minute." or "Couldn’t reach Chalk. Check your connection, then try again." instead of one generic line. The notices about offline changes read "Changes you made offline weren’t sent because the call changed." and its siblings in plain words, and still say expired changes are kept for up to 24 hours and that a failed save can be retried when storage is available.
