The call invite now lives in one place. A host who is alone at call start sees the call menu open with the QR code expanded, and the menu closes when someone joins. `CallReady` is no longer exported, and `ControlBar` no longer takes `shareUrl`, `callLinkOpen`, `onCallLinkToggle` or `onCopyError`; pass `callLinkOpen` and `onCallLinkToggle` to `SpaceHeader` or `SpaceView` instead. Record moved into the dock and turns red while recording. The weak-connection chip is a quiet pill that opens connection details, and the header no longer shows a recording pill: while recording, the call menu starts with a Recording row that shows the elapsed time, with Stop for people who manage recording. `SpaceHeader` takes `recordingSeconds` and `onStopRecording` for it. The call menu drops the keyboard shortcuts row (the ? key still opens them), and Copy diagnostics is an icon button. "Report a problem" shows whenever the app passes `onReportProblem`. The reactions button stays usable while reconnecting: the hand still works and emoji wait for the link. The people panel uses plain mic and camera icons and a host crown, and hides Mute everyone when you're alone. The end screen no longer shows the raw reference code; it goes into the problem report instead. The dock no longer hides while the pointer rests on it, and any click on the stage brings it back. A side column of shared content keeps people in view instead of folding them into +N, and on a phone the second face floats at the bottom right, clear of the big tile's menu. A cancelled screen-share picker no longer adds a share tile. Your own big share drops its repeated tag, and on narrow windows its bar moves to the bottom edge. A chat message's reaction bar opens at the far edge, away from the sender name and time, and the hidden attachment input is out of the tab order. The camera menu and the phone device sheets load on intent, so they open at full height. The knock notice is one row on wide screens, a long tile name ends in an ellipsis, and the phone dock fits at 200% zoom. A standalone `ControlBar` shows its media notice or command failure as a dismissible error toast, and `CommandErrorAlert` is no longer exported from `test-support`. Each half of a split dock button shows its own focus ring. On a phone or touch screen, a tile's More menu opens as a sheet. The tile signal chip uses the name tag's on-media colours.

A rejected call command whose reason is a bare server code, such as a failed recording start, now shows "That didn’t work. Try again in a moment." instead of the code.

The call theme uses the shared danger reds, so white text on Leave, Remove and Clear board holds 4.5:1 in both themes.
