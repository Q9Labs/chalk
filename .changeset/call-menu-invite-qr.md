The call menu now has a "Copy invite link" row and a QR row that opens a rounded QR code, and connection details show the last minute of latency, packet loss and bandwidth as charts. "Report a problem" is a top-level menu item that stays visible unless the server says reports are off. The invite card shows the QR code directly on the card. The client media state now carries `callStats`, the recent call-stats windows, so apps can draw their own charts.
