## Joining and admission

The React call joins directly with the supplied display name and device defaults. CallLimbo shows progress while joining. The web app collects a name before mounting the call; microphone and camera popovers remain available inside it.

### Admission policy

Operators can set two Space admission policies: `open` and `knock`. The retired `members_only` policy needs account authentication, which is no longer available. Existing Spaces with that policy stay closed until an operator explicitly changes it to `open` or `knock`; this release does not widen access automatically. A knock request appears in `participants.admissionQueue`; a Participant with `manageAdmission` can admit or deny it.

```ts
await client.participants.admit(requestId);
// or
await client.participants.deny(requestId);
```

Admission is distinct from identity. A Guest can be admitted to the current Episode without becoming a Member. A Member keeps standing Space access across Episodes.

<Callout tone="note" title="One entry surface">
  Entrance covers device preparation and waiting for admission in one place. Your product can style it or replace it with a custom panel while keeping the same `join` options.
</Callout>

Your backend can call `admitEpisodeParticipant` with `admission: "direct"` to admit a Participant and receive access without applying the Space’s `open`, `knock`, or `members_only` admission gate. Omit `admission` (or send `"policy"`) to retain policy-based admission; direct admission still requires `episodes:write` and all normal Tenant, Episode, role, and capacity checks.

### Joining on multiple devices

Admit a separate participant for each device. Reuse that device's admission key
for retries, and keep its access grants and leave operation scoped to its
participant. The optional `identityExternalId` admission field resolves a stable
person key within your tenant; use the same key for each of that person's devices.
Use either `identityExternalId` or an existing `identityId`.

The web SDK suppresses audio playback from your other devices. A new device starts
with its microphone off if another live device with the same identity is already
publishing microphone audio. Each device retains its own assigned role and
moderation capabilities. Attendance and lifecycle webhooks remain per participant.
